Required first. Four fields (name, status, currency, pricing model) let an agency create a client in under ten seconds. Name is the only blocker, and the message says exactly what to do. Business, niche, country, contact, tags, notes and logo sit behind Add details and can be filled later from the client page.

After creating. The new client opens on its Funnels tab with the empty state “No funnels yet” and two actions: new funnel, from a template. A brief confirmation appears, with Undo.

Pricing model. Choosing Retainer shows a Monthly retainer field (as in the details panel); Fixed, Hourly, Commission and Other show their own single field.
