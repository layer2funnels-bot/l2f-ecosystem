Book checkout
4320 × 4,824 · 7.2 MB		Top 88% of the page: almost all of it.MisleadingThe large white card says “CHECK OUT” with no form: the embedded checkout had not loaded when this screenshot was taken. In a 66 px window it reads as a blank or failed capture, and at 40% zoom it is a white square. Do not detect this with pixels: measured, the opt-in has more flat white (30%) than the checkout (21%). Fix at capture time: wait for embeds, then show the enlarged preview so a person can see it, with Re-capture and Upload one click away.
5 · Specification
PartSpec
Page window68 × 76, image area 66 × 65. Source crop 132 × 130 (2×), top-anchored, full width, height = width × 65/66. Never a centre crop: on an 8,000 px page it shows the middle of nowhere.
Peek296 × 300 (source 592 × 600), loaded on demand. Measured 42–56 KB.
Preview panelFull page at 272 wide in a scrolling viewport, with a draggable 66:65 frame that shows exactly what the window will show.
Low-detail tileBelow 50% zoom the object swaps to a 196 × 112 image tile (source 392 × 224). At 40% the 66 px window is 26 px; the wide tile stays recognisable.
Stored imageOne 1,200 px-wide JPEG per page (measured 298 / 376 / 123 KB against originals of 1.2 MB / 7.8 MB / 7.2 MB). The canvas only ever loads the ~5 KB window crop. Originals are not kept on the canvas.
Upload limitsPNG, JPG, WebP. Proposed: up to 25 MB and at least 800 px wide. Transparent PNGs are flattened on white.
6 · Anything hard for a human? checked against the design
Risk for a personHow the design answers itStatus
Too small to readThe window is for recognition, not reading, and says so by design. Peek on hover or focus, large preview in the panel. The name and type caption are always there, so the image never carries the meaning alone.Designed
Looks blank or failedCannot be detected reliably from pixels (measured). Capture waits for embedded forms; the panel shows the enlarged preview and a plain “Preview doesn’t look right?” link.Designed + engineering
Dark page vanishes into the dark canvasThe window keeps its light chrome and a 1 px #475569 border; images are flattened on white first.Designed
Drag or click?The whole object drags. Click selects. The image is never a link: Open page is an explicit button, so nobody leaves the app by accident.Designed
Keyboard and screen readersFocusing the object opens the peek; Esc closes it. Image text alternative: “name — type, uploaded screenshot”.Designed
TouchNo hover: tap selects and the panel shows the preview; long-press opens the peek.Designed
Heavy files slow the canvasOriginals are 1.2 MB / 7.8 MB / 7.2 MB. The canvas loads only the window crop; peek and panel load on demand.Engineering
Wrong or stale imageThe panel always states the source and original size. Replace and Re-capture are one click; the image is never replaced silently.Designed
Upload errorsPlain language with the next step (see the six states above). No codes.Designed
Client privacyPreviews can show real people and personal details. A “Show page previews” switch on the share link would help. Outside this board, so not changed.P2, not changed
