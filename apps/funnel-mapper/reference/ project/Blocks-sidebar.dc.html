DraggingThe tile lifts at 80% opacity and shows what will land. The block appears exactly where it is released, snapped to the grid, already selected.
Why this is simpler. One list, in this order: Search, Recent, this client’s products, then every category as one accordion with counts (like your live app). Only one category is open at a time and it shows 9 tiles before Show all. No filter chips, no tabs, no second navigation: the categories are the navigation. Tiles are your icons, logo above and label below, 3 per row.

Interaction. Drag a tile onto the map, or click to place it in the middle of the view. / focuses search from anywhere; Enter places the first result; Esc clears. Hovering a tile for 600 ms shows one line about it. The panel collapses to a 52 px rail of category icons; clicking one opens the panel on that category.

Where widgets live. In this same sidebar, as the last section, Widgets. Drag one onto the map (or click to place), then pick its scope and metrics in the inspector. Every widget already on the map is listed in the funnel panel under Widgets, so you can find, edit or remove them without hunting on the canvas.
