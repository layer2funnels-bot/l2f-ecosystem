Where it appears.
Funnel panel — A list of compact rows, ordered by impact. Replaces the separate Biggest leak and Model checks styles.
Block Analytics — The selected block’s findings, in a collapsed slot at the top. Invisible when empty.
Reports — Under each section header: what this section found.
Goals and Scenarios — The gap, and what a scenario changed.
The map — A marker on the block (the dashed ring already used for the biggest leak).
Rules. A finding always has evidence and a next step. It never says “AI”, never gives advice the numbers do not support, and the slot is not drawn when there are no findings. Every example here is something the product already knows: the leak from the map, a structural check, a goal gap. Later, richer findings use the same anatomy with nothing redesigned.
