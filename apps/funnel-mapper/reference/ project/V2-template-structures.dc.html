How to read these. Every block is the real object at small size: a page shows its window and caption, an offer its price tab, a message its capsule, an Action (Form Completed, Add to Cart, Schedule Meeting, Watch Video) its diamond. Dashed outlines are optional offers and downsells. Dashed orange lines are people who leave; dotted lines are skips and loop-backs. Block counts are counted from the drawings, not typed. Sample numbers ship with each template; the “Start with empty numbers” switch on the customize form clears them. Check each drawing against your Funnelytics and Russell originals.
