Never colour alone. At every level a line carries meaning with its style as well: solid continues, dashed orange leaves, dotted skips. A person who cannot see colour reads the same map.
Line labels. Near: every label. Mid: only leak labels, and the rate on hover. Far: none; thickness carries volume.
What never shrinks away. The page window is the last detail to go: pages stay recognisable until the shapes take over below 11% scale.
