Hit area. Only the page window (68 × 76). Title, numbers, caption, ports and the rest of the block never open the preview.
Placement. Below first, then above, then right, then left, choosing the side that covers the fewest blocks. Never over the hovered block or its ports. If no side fits, a compact 240px preview opens.
Timing. Opens after 400 ms on the window. Closes 150 ms after the pointer leaves it, and immediately if the pointer moves onto the preview itself so its buttons can be used.
Never in the way. Does not open while dragging, panning or zooming, or when a menu is open. Touch: long-press the window. Keyboard: focusing the window opens it; Esc closes it.
