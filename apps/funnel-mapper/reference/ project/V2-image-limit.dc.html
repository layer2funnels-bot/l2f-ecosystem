Where it applies
Page thumbnails, URL screenshots, client logos, workspace logo, custom block icons, template previews.
How it works
Resize to a sensible width, convert to WebP, lower the quality step by step until it fits under 100 KB. The result shows its final size.
When it cannot fit
The file is rejected with a plain message, and nothing is stored. The app never keeps an image over 100 KB.
