Every KPI shows before → after · + more
Baseline is one thing. It is the current map, always called Baseline (current map). The bar names the scenario being shown and what it is compared with. Nothing is identified by a time.
Before → after, in three places at once. The bar lists what changed; blocks carry a Δ chip; the strip shows each KPI as was / now / signed change. A person can answer “what changed, against what, by how much” without opening anything.
Motion is optional. Dots show flow; thickness and labels carry the same facts. With reduced motion there are no dots and nothing is lost. Numbers shown are from the scenarios list (Best case $9,960, 15.1x; Baseline $6,825, 11.3x). The full behaviour is in Simulation spec.
