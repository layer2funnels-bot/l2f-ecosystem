The decision, not just the number. The row answers: where are we against the target, against the plan, how far is the gap, and what must change. Every figure is computed from numbers the product already has (here: target $10,000, plan $7,485, actual $6,912; the gap is the target minus actual). Risk and Forecast have reserved slots that stay invisible until real data exists. No placeholder, no “coming soon”.
