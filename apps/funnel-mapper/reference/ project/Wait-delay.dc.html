Behaviour

Math-neutral. Everyone who arrives leaves — a delay never adds or removes people, so it doesn't change any total, rate or forecast.

Units: minutes, hours, days, weeks. The canvas label and subtitle update live from duration + unit ("Wait 30 min", "Wait 2 days", "Wait 3 wks").

Family: same capsule and dashed metric rule as Email / SMS, so sequences read as one timeline — but the clock icon marks it instantly as a pause.
