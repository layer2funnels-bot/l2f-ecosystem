Why four ports. A block can connect on any side, so a map can run left to right, top to bottom or loop back without bending lines around blocks. The ports are all alike. What a line means (Yes, No, Skip step, No data) is chosen on the line when you connect, and can be changed later by selecting it.

The old fixed Y and N ports are gone. Condition blocks keep their Yes and No chips, because there the two outcomes are the block’s job.
