1. Switch to Actual In the Plan control at the top. The map keeps its shape and shows the real numbers for the dates you chose, or “—” where nothing is entered.
2. Type it on the block Double-click any block: its first tab becomes Actual. Enter people in and people who moved on; the rate and the difference from Plan appear at once.
3. Enter a whole table Enter numbers opens a grid with one row per step for the chosen dates (Month, Week or Custom), with paste from a sheet.
4. Import CSV Same result from a spreadsheet export. Imported rows are tagged Actual, source CSV.
