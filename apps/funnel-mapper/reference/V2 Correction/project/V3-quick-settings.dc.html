How it behaves
Open. Double-click a block, or press Enter on a selected block. A single click only selects. It never opens while dragging or panning.
Place. Below the block, then above, right, left, whichever hides the fewest blocks. Never over the block, its ports or the next step. The popup is screen-sized and does not scale with canvas zoom.
Edit. Type or drag. Numbers on the canvas and the KPI strip update live. Enter applies, Esc reverts, Tab moves between fields. One undo step per session.
Close. Esc, a click outside, the ×, or opening another block's settings. Focus returns to the block.
More. More settings › opens the full inspector on its Details tab. Anything not in the popup (URL, preview, notes, style, plan, analytics) lives there.
Touch and phone. Long-press or tap Edit on a selected block. On phone it opens as a bottom sheet with 44px controls.
