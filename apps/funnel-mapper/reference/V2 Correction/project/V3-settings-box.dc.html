Tabs. Settings is always first and holds the few numbers people change most. Page (pages), Pricing (offers), Style and Notes follow. Everything here edits the block without opening the right panel.
Actions. Duplicate, Lock and Move to Trash sit in the footer of the box, so no separate toolbar floats over the block. Trash asks first.
Full panel. Open full panel › opens the right panel on the same block for anything not in the box: analytics for the step, plan per period, the full list of settings.
Behaviour. Double-click or Enter opens it. It opens below the block (then above, right, left), never over the next step. Enter applies, Esc reverts, Tab moves. Numbers on the canvas update live. On phone it is a bottom sheet.
