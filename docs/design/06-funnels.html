5 · Office → Funnels

One pattern for all four record types: list, record, edit, share with client, archive, restore, permanent delete. Funnel maps themselves stay in Funnel Mapper Pro; the link between them is a separate design that needs your approval.

Try: open a record, edit it and press Cancel, share it with a client, archive it, then open Archived and delete it. Switch to Viewer or Editor to see what changes.
